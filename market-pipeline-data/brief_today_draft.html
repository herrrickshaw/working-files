📊 Today's research: multi-factor framework extension (US)

Backtested on SEC EDGAR point-in-time data (5,016 tickers, 2016–2025, 9 rebalances). Educational/research only.

Earnings Yield + Sloan Ratio (EBIT/EV valuation + accrual-based earnings quality):
  • Earnings Yield top-20: +8.6% excess over universe, 8/9 years beat
  • Adding it on top of an already-quality-filtered pool (Piotroski F+ROCE) made results slightly worse (+7.1% excess, 6/9), not better — quality and cheapness don't simply stack
Growth & risk factors (P/FCF, FCF/EBITDA, Altman Z-Score, Reinvestment Rate×ROCE, 12-1 Momentum):
  • P/FCF (cheapest): +15.2% excess, 6/9 years beat
  • FCF/EBITDA (best cash conversion): +7.1% excess, 5/9
  • Altman Z-Score does NOT work as a ranking factor (0/9 years beat universe) — small-cap balance-sheet-thin names dominate the "safest" ranking without being genuinely safe. It DOES work as a gate: excluding distressed-zone names from the existing quality top-20 improved 5/9→7/9 years beating the universe
  • 12-1 Momentum alone: did not work in this sample (3/8 years beat)
