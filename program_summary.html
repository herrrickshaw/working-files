🌐 Cross-Market Screener Backtest — Program Summary

Point-in-time backtest of price + fundamental stock screens across 19 markets + the US, on a purpose-built dated-fundamentals & 10-year-price dataset. 2026-07-10. Research/education only — NOT investment advice.

One rule, three parts. (1) Price screens are regime-specific — buy weakness in the US & developed East Asia, buy strength elsewhere. (2) The Piotroski quality score pays outside the US and inverts inside it. (3) The strongest signal is the combination — a price screen gated by quality (F≥7) improves 13 of 19 markets.

③ Finding 2 — Quality pays, except in the US

Piotroski F≥7, point-in-time: US −3.9pp (inverted) vs rest-of-world +2.6pp median edge (positive in 7/10 deep-window markets). The US rewards value & mean-reversion; most other markets reward quality.

④ Finding 3 — The winner: price × quality

⑤ Deliverables & Reproducibility

  • Data + code: one repo market-screener-backtests — a single DuckDB warehouse, SQL access via warehouse.py, one clean commit, CI green (integrity + tests).
  • Cross-market backtests: backtest_fundamentals_all.py (Piotroski / Magic Formula / Quality / net-net) and backtest_combo_all.py (price × fundamental).
  • US point-in-time: backtest_piotroski_us.py, backtest_coffee_can_us.py, backtest_magic_formula_us.py, backtest_combo_us.py (SEC EDGAR history).
  • Collectors: local_collect.py, deep_prices.py, yf_fundamentals.py (curl_cffi, throttle-resistant) + a Google Colab notebook.
  • SSOT: exchange_reference.parquet — per-exchange ticker/collection-date freshness.
  • Shareables: an interactive findings artifact + a Word/PDF research summary.

⑥ Infrastructure notes

  • Throttle bypass: Yahoo blocks datacenter IPs; curl_cffi Chrome-impersonation + a global rate-limiter + retry-with-backoff made the full 20-market collection reliable.
  • Storage: consolidated 109 per-market parquets (~1.3 GB) → one DuckDB (~760 MB); history squashed to reclaim the old LFS objects.

⚠️ Educational/research only. NOT investment advice.
